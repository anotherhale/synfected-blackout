# BLACKOUT — SYNFECTED Franchise Alignment

## Source of truth
The franchise baseline is **Synfected GDD v0.2**, dated November 10, 2026. BLACKOUT is a product-specific experiment under SYNFECTED: RESISTANCE. This repository does not replace or modify that franchise document.

The original concept establishes a technology-infecting alien threat and a hacker resistance. GDD v0.2 refines the franchise into two distinct products: accessible Roblox Resistance and deeper standalone Syndicate. Resistance is a complete product with its own roadmap and quality bar.

The uploaded document titled **AI Game Development Feasibility** contains substantial earlier concept/gameplay notes. It is not treated here as a verified feasibility assessment or evidence of implementation capability.

## Inherits
SYNFECTED setting and Resistance identity; cyberpunk hacker-resistance atmosphere; alien intelligence/infected technology; accessible Roblox multiplayer; product distinction between Resistance and Syndicate; deterministic authoritative systems; age-appropriate constrained interactions; original creative expression.

## Does not inherit
Syndicate advanced economic simulation, SIN, persistent autonomous NPC careers/memories, player-run businesses/markets, blockchain/crypto, or the full Scout/Hardware Hacker/Software Hacker progression loop.

## Product-specific additions
BLACKOUT introduces a focused social-deduction loop with Infected, Sentinel, and Operative roles. This is a proposed Resistance experience, not a franchise-wide progression change. Hybrid cyberpunk UI is a BLACKOUT decision, not automatically a franchise-wide UI decision.

## Conflict governance
If a BLACKOUT proposal conflicts with GDD v0.2: identify the exact conflict; classify as product specialization or franchise-level change; record decision in ADR/design proposal; never silently rewrite the franchise baseline.
